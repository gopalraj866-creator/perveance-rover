## Description
On October 11, 2026 (sol 2,006), NASA's Perseverance rover hit 28.10 miles (45.22 km) on Mars, passing the 28.06 miles (45.16 km) set by Opportunity. It did it in about 5.5 years, versus nearly 15 for Opportunity. How? AutoNav, a self-driving system that has handled more than 90% of its distance.

In this video: the old champion, the sky-crane landing, record day, how AutoNav works, the original 12.4-mile plan, the route toward Idubi, the off-world leaderboard (Opportunity, Lunokhod 2, Curiosity, Apollo 17), and what's next.
